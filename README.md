# kallaidigitalsurvey

Modern, premium, responsive business website for **KallaiDigitalSurvey** — Land & Engineering Digital Survey Solutions.

## Features
- **Land Digital Survey**: Property boundary survey, land measurement, topographical & contour mapping.
- **Engineering Survey**: Building setting out, road survey, site survey, structural alignment & leveling.
- **Advanced Digital Survey**: GPS/GNSS RTK positioning, Total Station mapping, Drone aerial survey, 3D DTM & GIS mapping.
- **Interactive Components**: Filterable project gallery masonry grid, fullscreen Lightbox modal, animated scroll counters, FAQ accordion, validated quote form, and sticky navbar.
