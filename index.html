FAQ

Common questions about our survey services.

What is a digital land survey?
A digital land survey uses electronic distance measurement, GNSS RTK, and total stations to create highly accurate electronic maps and 3D terrain models.
How accurate is a Total Station survey?
Total Station instruments provide sub-millimeter angular and distance measurements, ideal for engineering layouts and property boundaries.
How long does a land survey take?
Standard site surveys take 1–2 days of field data collection plus 24 hours for CAD drawing drafting.
Do you provide digital survey reports?
Yes! We provide complete AutoCAD DWG, PDF contour maps, 3D point clouds, and government-compliant documentation.
What areas do you serve?
