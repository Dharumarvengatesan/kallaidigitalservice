FAQ

Common questions about our survey services.

What is a digital land survey?
A digital land survey uses modern electronic instruments like Total Station and satellite GPS/GNSS receivers to measure land boundaries, elevation, and terrain points with sub-centimeter precision, delivering exact CAD drawings and DWG digital blueprints.
How accurate is a Total Station survey?
A Total Station measures angles to within 1 to 5 arcseconds and distances to within 1-2 millimeters per kilometer, making it extremely reliable for building setting out and boundary demarcation.
How long does a land survey take?
Standard plot and site surveys typically take 1 to 2 days for field data collection, with final CAD drawing delivery within 24 to 48 hours.
Do you provide digital survey reports?
Yes, we provide complete digital survey reports including AutoCAD (.DWG), PDF maps, Excel coordinate tables, and 3D contour elevation models.
